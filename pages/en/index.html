About the RuneWorld Server

RuneWorld is a vanilla Minecraft server with a welcoming atmosphere, reliable hosting, friendly players, and helpful administrators. Our server runs on Minecraft Realms, giving players uninterrupted access from any country. The Microsoft hosting keeps latency low around the world. We hold events, build mega-projects, and continue developing the server infrastructure. The server is built around annual seasons; the current one is Season 5. Join us and become part of our friendly community!

Anti-cheat

RuneWorld uses an advanced anti-cheat system to ensure fair play. We use modern detection methods to identify and block players who try to gain an advantage through prohibited modifications or cheats. The system is updated regularly to counter new threats and exploits. Our goal is to give every player an equal, enjoyable experience without fraud or unfair tactics. Join RuneWorld and play with confidence, knowing that fair play is protected.
