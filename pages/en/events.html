RuneWorld Events

The event will take place in Minecraft Java, but rewards will be issued on RuneWorld 4. A year ago, Redwood fell silent. The forest stood motionless when something awakened in the heart of the old bunker, setting off outdated ammunition. A group of journalists was accidentally caught in the blast zone. Two survived. Three, driven by the fire of the automated security system, stepped into a portal gaping in the darkness below. The military arrived quickly. They sealed the perimeter. The park was declared a dead zone. Mark the ranger was taken away under guard; command believed he knew more than he was saying. The first reconnaissance team returned with empty eyes. They could barely speak, but they had to. There were bodies inside. Bloodless. Limbs broken as if they had been dropped from a great height. Some were recent. The missing journalists. Then the others came: those who clean, those who build, those who guard. Reconnaissance pushed farther and saw a wall. A sector. Foreign territory inside an unknown world. Two thousand metres by two thousand, like a square cut out of reality. Now it is your turn. You are the first assault squad. The entry point has already been selected. Do not count on a way back. Complete the mission. Do not let us down.
