Moytashbud Clan

Moytashbud is a company that builds prefabricated apartment blocks on the RuneWorld server. The company has been building since 2023. Its first project was a 14-storey K-14 series building completed in just over two weeks. It required a large quantity of materials and included a water supply system and a lift. Unfortunately, the building was destroyed by shelling during a coup. The next project was a nine-storey Series 96 building completed in just one week. It also housed a trading hall with villagers and donkeys. Later, a nine-storey 1-464-20A series building was constructed. Building took one week, after which it entered service. It also included a water supply system and a lift. At the end of August 2025, a nine-storey Series 113-87 building was completed in a record three days thanks to enormous help from RuneCraft2287. The building proved very reliable, and anyone can now purchase an apartment on any floor.
